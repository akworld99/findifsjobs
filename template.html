      How the ranking works. Each partner gets points for being IFS-only (3) or an ERP boutique (1.5), a recent growth signal such as a merger, new tier or new market (2), being founded since 2016 (1.5), IFS awards from 2023 to 2025 (1 each, up to 2), partner tier (Elite 1.5, Premier 1, Specialized 0.5) and a home base in the region (3). Facts come from the IFS partner directory, IFS press releases and each firm's own site. It is an editorial ranking, not an IFS one.
